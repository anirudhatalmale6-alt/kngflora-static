about us.

KNGflora is a global trading company in the horticulture industry. It is a department of KNGAsia, registered in HongKong and owned by Belgians, with over 12 years experience in China.

  • Mission: Twofold, in both directions.
    1. To simplify trade between Chinese (small) growers and help them expand by selling their products directly to buyers all over the world.
    2. To supply horticulture products from all over the world to the Chinese market.
  • Vision: putting win-win-win as goal, become a reliable and important player in the horticulture trade between Asia and the rest of the world.
